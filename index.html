Tips

  • Each measurement is in micrometres (µm). Q is the length divided by the width for each spore, and the average Q is the mean of those ratios.
  • Tap the two edges of the spore across its longest axis for length, and across its widest part for width.
  • Calibration uses the two ruler taps and the number of units between them, so tap two marks you can count.
